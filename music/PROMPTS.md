# Music prompts (I generate the music myself, e.g. in Suno)

Fill the brackets for each video. Length = final video length + 3 s.
Always: instrumental, no vocals, steady energy (it sits under speech), clean ending.

## Template
> [Genre] instrumental at [BPM] BPM, [mood words]. Instruments: [3-5 instruments].
> Steady, driving energy that sits under a spoken voiceover, with a small lift at
> [timestamp ~60%] and a confident final hit at [length]s. No vocals, no vocal chops,
> no big drops, no long intro (music starts on beat 1). Length [N] seconds.

## Proven presets
- **Paper-cut / local business (upbeat, handmade)**: indie-pop, 100-110 BPM, foot stomps,
  hand claps, plucky muted electric guitar, warm bass, light glockenspiel.
- **Trust / premium service**: warm cinematic pop, 90-96 BPM, soft piano, muted kick,
  airy pads, subtle strings swell at the end.
- **Hype / before-after**: modern trap-pop, 140 BPM (half-time feel), punchy 808, snaps,
  bright synth pluck. Keep the hi-hats soft so they don't fight the voice.
- **Storm / urgency (roofing hail season)**: tense pulse, 120 BPM, low synth ostinato,
  ticking percussion, resolves to a major chord for the CTA.

After you send me a track: I save it to `music/` with an `index.json` entry and run an
instrumental check (whisper should hear only "(music)").
