# Outreach (harun.motion)

## Rules
- First DM: **no selling, no price, no "I offer services"**. Just the free edit.
- Send the **video file** in the DM (not a link), then the message right after.
- Casual, short, one paragraph, a little emoji at most. Use the person's first name if known.
- If the video makes a claim they didn't say (e.g. "FREE consultation"), ask them to
  confirm it in the DM.
- Only after a positive reply: "happy to do the same for your other reels anytime 👍".
- Log every lead in `leads/leads.csv`; never DM the same business twice with a new edit
  unless they replied.

## DM template (fill the brackets)
> Hey [Name]! I loved your [topic] video from [yesterday / this week], so I re-edited it
> in a [style] style with a stronger hook, new captions and tighter pacing to keep people
> watching longer. It's completely free, no strings attached, and you're welcome to post it
> on your socials. If you'd like anything changed, just let me know and I'll fix it.
> [Optional claim check: I also put "FREE consultation" on it, so tell me if that's not
> right for you guys] 🙌

## Lead statuses (leads.csv `status`)
`edited` → `dm_sent` → `replied` → `posted` (they used it) → `client` / `no_reply` / `declined`
